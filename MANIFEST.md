# Phase-1 release manifest

## Included

- AURA temporal encoder and cross-motor attention architecture.
- Self-supervised training script and CSV data contract.
- Trained alpha/kappa checkpoint.
- Locomotion policy checkpoints for walking, circular locomotion, and boxing behaviors.
- Dependency list, license, and reproducibility notes.

## Deliberately withheld

- Locomotion policy training runs, observation builders, and training configurations.
- Raw and processed recordings, rosbag/MCAP files, and private metadata.
- Isaac Lab robot assets and simulator task code.
- Robot transport, hardware drivers, torque-command deployment, and endurance-test scripts.

The withheld components are planned for later staged releases.
